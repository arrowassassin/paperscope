Kintsugi Tools is the studio behind Kintsugi and Paperscope. We build focused software that keeps your work on your machine and under your control.

Principles

The defaults matter

A tool should be useful without asking for ownership of your data, your attention, or your workflow.

Local-first

Core workflows run on your machine. Your documents, code, indexes, and policies stay with you; no account is required.

No telemetry

No analytics, tracking, or quiet phone-home behavior. Network access is reserved for explicit actions such as downloading an installer or model.

Permissive licenses

Inspect, modify, and build on the code. Kintsugi is MIT licensed; Paperscope is available under MIT OR Apache-2.0.

Design philosophy

Quiet software, explicit control.

We prefer deterministic behavior, inspectable data, exact citations, sensible defaults, and clear escape hatches. Tools should explain what they are doing, work offline where possible, and get out of the way.
